📖 For Teachers — Lesson Plan

🏅 Sports Day — Year 5 English

Theme: World of Self, Family and Friends — Sports & Activities

Focus: Writing (past simple tense)

Duration: 60 minutes

Objectives: Talk about sports using present simple & continuous. Write about a sports event using past simple.

🎬 Set Induction (10 min)

Teacher plays a sports video (football, badminton, swimming, basketball). Pupils identify each sport and raise their hand if they play it.

Teacher asks: "Do you play ___? How often do you play?"

Teacher: Facilitator
Students: Viewer & Responder

📸 Pre-lesson (10 min)

Teacher presents sports vocabulary & equipment using flashcards.

Group activity: Match sports to equipment on a poster.

High readiness: Add 2 extra sports
Low readiness: Use picture bank with labelled equipment

📝 Lesson Development (25 min)

"A Day at the Sports Day" — Differentiated reading & writing.

Level A: Past simple with time phrases
Level B: Past simple, simpler vocab
Level C: Picture-supported, key verbs highlighted

Writing: High: 6-8 sentences. Intermediate: guided frame. Low: cloze passage.

🎤 Post-lesson (10 min)

Sports Reporters: Pupils read writing like a news reporter. Audience gives Two Stars and a Wish. High-readiness presenters answer audience questions.

🎭 Closure (5 min)

Sports Charades: Teacher calls a sport, one pupil acts it out silently. Class guesses. 4 rounds. Recap past simple tense.

🤖 Station A: C Canva AI

Poster Design (20 min): Canva AI text-to-design. Prompt: "Design a sports day poster with: We ran, we jumped, we won!" Pupils add 3-5 past tense sentences.

🤖 Station B: G Gemini AI

Writing Coach (20 min): "Help me write 4 sentences about my Sports Day using past tense." Low readiness: "Give me 3 simple sentences." High readiness: "Check which verbs need past tense."

⚡ Station C: M Microsoft AI

Reading & Pronunciation (20 min): Copilot/Translator. Type a sentence, hear it spoken. Ask: "Correct my grammar: Yesterday I play football."

👧👦 For Pupils — Your Sports Day Lesson

🏅 Welcome to Sports Day!

Today we are going to learn about sports and write about Sports Day.

You will learn to:

  • Talk about sports you play
  • Write about a sports day using past tense (yesterday, last Saturday)
  • Use AI tools to help you!

⚽ Let's Watch Some Sports!

Your teacher will show a video. Look at the sports and raise your hand if you play them!

Questions to think about:

  • "Do you play football?"
  • "How often do you play?"
  • "What is your favourite sport?"

🎒 Sports Equipment Matching

Match the sport to its equipment!

  • ⚽ Football → Boots & Ball
  • 🏸 Badminton → Racket & Shuttlecock
  • 🏀 Basketball → Hoop & Ball
  • 🏊 Swimming → Goggles & Cap
  • 🚴 Cycling → Helmet & Bike

📖 Read About Sports Day

Your teacher will give you a story about a school Sports Day.

Look for past tense words:

  • "Last Saturday, the Sports Day began at 8 am."
  • "First, we played football."
  • "Then we ran a race."
  • "Finally, we celebrated our winners!"

✏️ Write Your Own Sports Day Story

Now it's your turn! Write about a sports day you remember.

Sentence starters:

  • "Last year, our school held a Sports Day..."
  • "First, we played..."
  • "Then, I ran in the..."
  • "I felt happy because..."

🎤 Be a Sports Reporter!

Read your writing to the class like a TV sports reporter!

Stand at the front and read loudly and clearly.

Your friends will give you Two Stars and a Wish — two things you did well and one thing to improve.

🎭 Sports Charades

Your teacher calls out a sport. One person acts it out silently. The class guesses!

No talking — only actions! Can your friends guess your sport?
