LEARNING STANDARDS

LEARNING OBJECTIVES
At the end of the lesson, pupils will be able to talk about sports they play using present simple for routines and present continuous for current activities. Pupils will be able to write a short paragraph describing a sport event or experience using past simple tense.

ACTIVITIES
═══════════════════════════════════════════

Set Induction — 10 min
[Differentiated Content] Teacher plays a short video clip of different sports (football, badminton, swimming, basketball). Pupils identify each sport and raise their hand if they play it. Teacher asks "Do you play ___?" and "How often do you play?" to introduce the topic.
Teacher: Facilitator — plays video; asks about sports experiences
Students: Viewer & Responder — identify sports; answer personal questions

Pre-lesson — 10 min
Teacher presents sports vocabulary and equipment using flashcards (racket, ball, bat, shuttlecock, net, helmet, gloves). [Differentiated Content] Pupils work in groups to match sports with their equipment on a group poster. High-readiness groups add 2 extra sports with equipment. Low-readiness groups use a picture bank with labelled equipment.
Teacher: Instructor — presents sports equipment vocabulary; guides group poster activity
Students: Matcher — match sports to equipment in groups at own level

Lesson Development — 25 min
[Differentiated Process] A Day at the Sports Day: Pupils read a short text about a school Sports Day. Three levels of text: Level A includes past simple with time phrases (Last Saturday, the Sports Day began at 8 am. First, we played...). Level B includes past simple with simpler vocabulary. Level C has a picture-supported text with key verbs highlighted. [Differentiated Product] Pupils then write about their own Sports Day or a sports experience. High-readiness pupils write 6-8 sentences using past simple and sequence words. Intermediate pupils use a guided writing frame. Low-readiness pupils complete a cloze passage about Sports Day.
Teacher: Facilitator — distributes tiered reading texts and writing scaffolds; circulates to support
Students: Reader & Writer — read differentiated text; write about own sports experience

Post-lesson — 10 min
Sports Reporters: Pupils volunteer to read their writing to the class like a sports reporter. Each presenter stands at the front and reads from their work. The audience claps and gives two stars and a wish (two positive points, one suggestion). [Differentiated Product] High-readiness presenters answer 1-2 questions from the audience.
Teacher: Moderator — sets up reporter podium; guides stars-and-wish feedback
Students: Presenter & Audience — present sports report; give structured feedback

Closure — 5 min
[Differentiated Product] Sports Charades: Teacher calls out a sport. One pupil acts it out silently. The class guesses. High-readiness pupils act out with added rules; low-readiness pupils guess from picture prompts. Quick-fire 4 rounds. Teacher recaps writing focus.
Teacher: Facilitator — leads sports charades; recaps writing goals
Students: Participant — act out or guess sports

REMEDIAL INSTRUCTION (AKTIVITI PEMULIHAN)
═══════════════════════════════════════════
Three AI-powered stations rotate every 20 minutes. Each station targets the same writing objective (past simple tense for sports experiences) through a different AI tool.

─── Station A: Canva AI — Design Your Sports Day Poster (20 min) ───
Pupils use Canva AI (canva.com) to create a Sports Day poster describing what happened. Type prompts like "Design a sports day poster with the words: We ran, we jumped, we won" — Canva AI generates a poster with matching visuals. Pupils then add 3-5 sentences about their own sports experience using past simple tense.
AI integration: Canva AI's text-to-design generates sport illustrations and poster layouts instantly. Pupils who struggle with writing can describe their experience orally and use the visuals as cues.
High use of visual/verbal aids | Peer-assisted learning | Explicit Instruction

─── Station B: Gemini AI — Sports Day Story Coach (20 min) ───
Pupils open gemini.google.com and use the prompt: "I am a Year 5 student. Help me write 4 sentences about my school Sports Day using past tense." Gemini generates a sample, then asks the pupil questions ("What sport did you play? Did you win?"). Pupils write their own version in their book with Gemini's scaffolding.
For lower readiness: Use prompt "Give me 3 simple sentences about Sports Day with pictures described."
For higher readiness: Use prompt "Check my sentences and tell me which verbs should be in past tense."
AI integration: Gemini provides instant, patient, one-on-one writing support — no embarrassment for struggling writers.

─── Station C: Microsoft AI — Reading & Pronunciation Practice (20 min) ───
Pupils use Microsoft Translator (translator.microsoft.com) or Microsoft Copilot (copilot.microsoft.com) to:
  1. Type a sport sentence and hear it spoken aloud (pronunciation model)
  2. Use Reading Progress (if available in class) to read a short sports text aloud
  3. Ask Copilot: "Correct my sentence: 'Yesterday I play football'"
Microsoft AI's text-to-speech helps pupils hear correct sentence structure and pronunciation before they write.
AI integration: Microsoft Translator's immersive reader and text-to-speech build reading fluency alongside writing.
